Research Interests

My research aims to enable trustworthy decision-making and rigorous scientific discovery through causally intelligent AI. I develop methods that strengthen causal reasoning in large language models, helping them understand underlying mechanisms, draw conclusions supported by evidence, and recognize when that evidence is insufficient. Building on these capabilities, I explore how AI can advance scientific discovery by integrating existing knowledge, generating new hypotheses, and testing them through new experiments.
